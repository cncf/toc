# kcp - Governance Review - 2026-11-19
 
What follows is a governance review for the [kcp](https://kcp.io/) project.
The review was conducted as part of due diligence for kcp's application to move
to CNCF's Incubation level.
 
**The review primarily consists of an audit on the project's
self-assessment in their Incubation application.**
 
- Incubation application / due diligence: <https://github.com/cncf/toc/issues/1909>
- Governance review issue: <https://github.com/cncf/toc/issues/1914>

### Must-Fix Items
 
**The following issues have been identified that need to be resolved before
Incubation:**
 
1. **List all subprojects.** The kcp-dev organization hosts several active
   repositories (for example `kcp-operator`, `api-syncagent`, `init-agent`,
   `multicluster-provider`, `helm-charts` and the monorepo's published
   libraries), but no single document lists them with their owners and status.
2. **Add contact information to `MAINTAINERS.md`.** The list has names, GitHub
   handles, domains and affiliations, but no contact details. One affiliation
   is shown as `-` and should say `Independent` if that is the case.
3. **Keep the public meeting calendar up to date.** The docs point to the
   [CNCF community group](https://ocgroups.dev/cncf/group/ktzun36) for meeting
   dates, but it lists no event after August 14, 2025, and no upcoming events,
   while the meeting notes show meetings roughly every two weeks through
   November 2026.
### Points of Excellence
 
**The following aspects of governance are exemplary, and can be referenced as
examples for other projects to copy:**
 
- **A clear AI-assisted contribution policy.** The
  [contributing guide](https://docs.kcp.io/kcp/main/contributing/) explains
  that humans remain responsible for AI-assisted contributions and that AI
  tools cannot sign off on the DCO (Developer Certificate of Origin). Other
  projects could reuse this text.
- **Useful meeting notes.** The
  [community meeting notes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PrEhbmq1WfxFv1fTikDBZzXEIJkUWVHdqDFxaY1Ply4)
  link the Code of Conduct, governance and contributing guide at the top, and
  record decisions and their outcomes, such as the mailing-list vote on the
  official "kcp" spelling.
### Areas for Improvement
 
**Over the next year, the project should work on the following issues to improve
its governance, these are considered non-blocking:**
 
- **Name the emeritus maintainers.** `MAINTAINERS.md` says former maintainers
  "could (and should)" be given emeritus status. The original approvers
  (`@ncdc`, `@stevekuznetsov`, `@davidfestal`) were removed from `OWNERS` in
  2023 but are not recognized anywhere. Listing them closes the loop on the
  maintainer lifecycle.
- **Fill out the contributor ladder.** The contributing guide names four roles
  (Contributor, Approver, Subproject Maintainer, Maintainer), but only
  Maintainer has written criteria. Add how someone becomes an org member,
  an Approver and a Subproject Maintainer, and how those roles are removed.
- **Plan for employer concentration.** Five of ten maintainers work for
  Kubermatic. This is not a requirement at Incubating, and a simple majority
  still needs a vote from outside Kubermatic, but one employer can block any
  2/3 vote (governance changes, maintainer removal). Consider a per-employer
  voting cap, as several CNCF projects use, before Graduation.
### Governance Summary
 
kcp is governed by a **Maintainer Council** made up of all maintainers (ten
today), defined in
[GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/kcp-dev/kcp/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) and
listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](https://github.com/kcp-dev/kcp/blob/main/MAINTAINERS.md).
Day-to-day decisions are made by lazy consensus. Votes are held on the
developer mailing list, at the community call, or through PR approvals. Most
votes need a simple majority of all maintainers. Governance changes and
maintainer removal need a two-thirds majority.
 
Two delegated teams exist: a **Security Response Team** (three maintainers) and
a **GitHub Admin Team** (four maintainers, not all from one employer).
Subprojects may add **Subproject Maintainers**, and maintainers may appoint
**Approvers** for parts of the code.
 
kcp was started at Red Hat, joined the CNCF Sandbox in 2023, and has since moved
to a multi-company maintainer group (Kubermatic, SAP, NVIDIA, IBM, Clyso, and
one independent).
