## Agenda

1. Welcome and what the initiative wants to achieve
2. Deliverable direction, with the Cloud Native Agentic Standards as the starting point
3. Workstreams and terms of reference
4. Contributor mapping

## Discussion

Vincent gave the background: the CNCF has been working on cloud native agent standards since May 2026, interest has moved from experimentation to enterprise agent development, and CNCF's role is to bridge foundations working on agent standards, data governance and operating standards. Participants introduced themselves and their areas of interest. Vincent walked through the shift of AI infrastructure on Kubernetes from batch scheduling to inference to agentic workloads, and the need to coordinate with existing work, in particular the Kubernetes Agentic Network working group and Gateway API. Victor suggested a mind map of related projects and dependencies.

Vincent proposed moving away from a traditional whitepaper toward maintaining standards as living documents in Git, because papers are out of date by the time they ship. Pavan raised the initiative's long-term standing and proposed tagging the document as release versions so each milestone is citable while the work continues. Vincent agreed.

## Decisions

- Operationalize and extend the Cloud Native Agentic Standards rather than write a new standalone whitepaper.
- Maintain the result as a GitHub-based living document updated through pull requests, with tagged release versions.
- Five workstreams aligned with the standards; lazy consensus; two-week reassignment for stalled work; biweekly Thursday sessions.
